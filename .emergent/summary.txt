<product_requirements>
The user requested a WhatsApp bot for a real estate agency to manage messages. Initial requirements included: official WhatsApp Business API integration, comprehensive bot functionalities, AI intelligence (Claude Sonnet 4), and guidance on API credentials.

Over time, requirements expanded to include:
- **Client Management:** Collect name, surname, phone, email, search criteria, budget, mortgage details, or selling information.
- **AI Conversation Flow:** Prevent repetitive introductions, handle selling inquiries (location, previous agency, valuation appointment), and provide a final privacy consent message.
- **Data Visualization:** Frontend sections for properties, messages, appointments, clients, and valuations.
- **Bot Customization:** Ability to change bot name and agency name, and control auto-responses for new contacts only.
- **UI Enhancements:** Delete functionality for clients, a evaluated checkbox for valuations, and an image upload (URL-based) section for properties.
- **Deployment:** Initially, a local hybrid setup for the WhatsApp bot was implemented due to environment constraints, then a move to online hosting (Railway) was requested, eventually transitioning from Baileys to Twilio WhatsApp API for stability.
- **Theming:** Ability to change the application's primary color from settings.
- **Mac Launcher:** An application to easily launch the bot on Mac.
</product_requirements>
